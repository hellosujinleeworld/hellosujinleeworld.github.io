Sujin Lee is a postdoctoral researcher at the [Safe Transportation Research and Education Center (SafeTREC)](https://safetrec.berkeley.edu/) at the University of California, Berkeley. Her research lies at the intersection of transportation, urban analytics, and data science, with a focus on understanding how people, transportation systems, and the built environment interact. She leverages large-scale mobility, telematics, spatial, and behavioral data, together with machine learning and statistical methods, to study transportation safety, travel demand and mobility, and urban systems. Her work aims to develop data-driven approaches to better understand transportation behavior and support safer, more efficient, equitable, and resilient cities.
